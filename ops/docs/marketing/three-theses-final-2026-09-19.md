## Indie Hackers

Title:
Agent harnesses must evolve. Three theses.

Link: https://helmdeck.de/?utm_source=indiehackers&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=launch&utm_content=launch-story

Text:

AI has written code for years. Since Fable, it is clear that no human will ever write better code again. The gap is wide now and it will not close, the same way no chess player caught up with DeepMind. Writing code by hand is over. Nobody who has tried it goes back.

That was the first shift. Three more are coming. Once you have tried each one, there is no way back either.

1. Mobile is the interface. It has been since the iPhone. Nobody should sit at a desk to fill out a form. You should be free to do what you want, when you want, wherever you are. The agent belongs at the desk. You do not.
This is how I shipped HelmDeck. I never touched the Play Store listing, the App Store listing, the certificates, the procurement. The agent did all of it in the browser. I set the vision and cleared the blockers, from wherever I was. The desk was for the machine.

2. Goals replace prompts. AI can plan across days now. It does not need the next instruction. It needs the destination. You set the goal, the budget and the deadline. The path is not your problem anymore.
Every week I set the resources. HelmDeck lays out timeline and scope. It knows my velocity and tells me what fits. It pushes back when it learns something I did not know, like an Android review that takes fourteen days. It found my testers on Reddit. I never asked for any of that step by step. I set the goal.

3. Teams, not individuals. Today one person drives one AI, and that person is the bottleneck. Nothing hard was ever solved alone. Agents will be run by teams, and every decision will land with the one person who should make it.
I work with a friend in another time zone. When I go to sleep, he takes the helm. He sees at once what moved and what is waiting on him. Two people, one goal, and the agents never stop in between.

I built HelmDeck to test these three theses.

Teams and agents at one helm, facing what comes next. You steer from whatever is in your hand, wherever you are. Your team solves the problems, with AI at their side. Work passes from one to the next without friction. Budget, scope and timeline, always in sight.

This is not a tool for typing faster. This is how work is going to feel.
