# Product context: helmdeck.de (marketing site)

register: brand

## What HelmDeck is
Mobile AI orchestration for teams. HelmDeck runs on the team's own PC with the Claude
subscription they already have; agents (Claude Code, verified live) work at the desk while
people steer from the phone: answer questions, approve, redirect. Board of cards, goals
instead of prompts, one decision lands with the one person who should make it.

## Audience
Teams running Claude Code (founders, small dev and agency teams) who want agents to keep working
while they are away from the desk. Technical, sceptical of hype, read English first, German second.

## The page's job
Show how it works in one screen (real screenshots, phone + desktop) and get the visitor to the
right download (platform-aware installer, App Store, Google Play). Then the three theses:
Mobile is the interface / Goals replace prompts / Teams, not individuals.

## Hard rules
- Copy is owner-owned: headline "Mobile AI orchestration for teams." and the three theses stay.
  Design work tightens layout, not words. No dashes in copy. Name Claude Code; never claim Codex works.
- English default, German toggle complete. Watch and glasses stay on the page, smaller.
- Hero (headline, subline, visual, download CTA) fits without scrolling at 1366x768, 1440x900,
  1742x980, 1920x1080 and 390x844; enforced by check_layout.py in this folder.
- Do not touch waitlist forms, KV, export, /health, analytics beacon.
- Screenshots are real app captures (currently German UI; no English set exists in the store screenshots).

## Look
Dark canvas, Schibsted Grotesk, blue accent, the app's aurora backdrop. Reference lane: Linear,
Raycast, Warp product-led dark pages. No decorative gimmicks; the product screenshot is the imagery.
